On the next page, InkDebug follows each line of a calculation. It stops at the first wrong step, shows the correction, and keeps the original work beside it.

For a new visual, InkMind reads the selected equation and turns it into a graph with working controls. Here the coefficient is three, the x term is zero, and the constant is negative one. Change the coefficient and the curve responds.
